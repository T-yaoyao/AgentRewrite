2. Plan Characteristics
- Scan type transitions: The original plan used Parallel Index Scan on item as the driving table, with Bitmap Heap/Bitmap Index Scans probing store_sales and web_sales. The rewritten plan replaces these with a Parallel Seq Scan on date_dim as the driver (leveraging the highly selective d_year = 2001 AND d_qoy IN {1,2,3} filter), followed by Index Scans on idx_store_sales_sold_date and idx_web_sales_sold_date. This flips the join direction to date-driven fact-table access, which is more efficient for the time-window predicate.
- Join algorithm refinements: The original plan's final stage consisted of a chain of five Nested Loops over six CTE scans, with expensive row-expression Join Filters comparing quarter-over-quarter ratios. The rewritten plan replaces this entire chain with a single Hash Join (Hash Cond on county + year), plus a small residual Join Filter for the ratio comparisons. The hash-based equi-join on (ca_county, d_year) eliminates the repeated linear re-scanning of the same CTE result.
- Elimination of explicit sorting and intermediate results: The original plan materialized two CTEs (each internally containing a Sort + Gather Merge) and then re-scanned them six times via CTE Scan nodes. The rewritten plan eliminates the CTE materialization entirely: conditional aggregation (CASE WHEN inside sum()) computes all three quarters in one pass, so the six CTE scans and their associated intermediate result sets disappear. Remaining sorts are minimal (two tiny sorts feeding GroupAggregate plus the final top-level Sort), compared to the original's multi-branch sort structure.

3. Resource Utilization
- Memory usage: The original plan required memory to materialize two CTEs and to hash/sort their rows for repeated access. The new plan introduces one small Hash build (over the ~1-row web-sales aggregate) and two small aggregate sorts, substantially reducing peak memory pressure.
