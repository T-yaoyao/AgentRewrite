**Understanding the semantics:**
- `GROUPING SETS((SAL, SAL > 1000), ())` groups by (SAL, SAL>1000) and grand total
- `GROUPING(SAL, SAL > 1000)` returns a bitmask: 0 means both SAL and SAL>1000 are grouped (detail row), 3 means both are rollup (grand total row)
- `g_0_f_1`: GROUPING=0 AND SAL>1000 IS TRUE → detail rows where SAL>1000
- `g_3`: GROUPING=3 → grand total rows only

**Applying FILTER_REDUCE_EXPRESSIONS:**
- `SAL > 1000 IS TRUE` simplifies to `SAL > 1000` (since SAL is INTEGER NOT NULL, no NULLs)
- So `g_0_f_1` becomes: `GROUPING(SAL, SAL > 1000) = 0 AND SAL > 1000`
